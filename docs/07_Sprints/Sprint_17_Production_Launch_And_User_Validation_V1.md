# Sprint 17 Production Launch And User Validation V1

Status: Planned sequence — blocked by Sprint 16 owner release decision
Prepared: 2026-08-19

## Intake Decision

### 1. Mainline fit

ZEROON needs real acquisition, activation, return, and trust evidence before
expanding features or physical delivery. Production and App Store availability
create access to users; they are not themselves evidence that the product is
valuable.

### 2. Drift risk

Launch validation can drift into vanity download counts, private-content
analytics, notification growth hacking, therapy positioning, or changing
multiple product variables during one cohort. Small samples must not be
presented as stable retention proof.

### 3. Recommended abstract capability

**Measured public learning loop**: acquire an identifiable adult audience,
separate channel performance from product activation and return behavior, and
pair content-free cohort metrics with interviews that never request private
Record disclosure.

### 4. Roadmap decision

Plan Sprint 17 after Sprint 16. Production deployment, App Store submission,
public release, promotion, participant contact, and external communication each
remain explicitly owner-authorized actions.

### 5. Planning acceptance criteria

- acquisition, activation, return, and trust have separate denominators;
- the first approximately 20 adults establish stability before expansion;
- expansion toward 50-100 occurs only after blocking failures are contained;
- small or immature cohorts produce directional observations, not claims;
- product promise and core flow remain stable during a measured cohort;
- research and evidence remain content-free and consent-controlled.

## Sprint Goal

Release one approved production/App Store version, bring enough relevant users
to produce meaningful product evidence, and decide independently whether
acquisition, activation, return value, and trust should Continue, Reshape, or
Stop.

## Evidence Layers

### Acquisition

- product-page impressions and views;
- downloads and download-to-authentication conversion;
- channel, audience, and promise learning without private-content tracking.

### Activation

- authentication and encounter completion;
- state selection and first valid Record;
- Archive or Record Detail review;
- understanding of Memory and AI-context controls.

### Return value

- D1 and D7 only for mature cohorts;
- week-two Record behavior when mature;
- Archive, Record Detail, Memory, and continuity-cue review;
- user-described reasons for returning or stopping.

### Trust

- Memory, delete, export, support, and app-protection discoverability;
- privacy concern, consent confusion, deletion demand, and complaint themes;
- no private Record text in analytics, admin, or research notes.

## Acceptance Criteria

- release version and deployed version are verified rather than inferred;
- store promise matches the shipped core loop;
- production blockers have accountable response and rollback;
- metrics preserve numerator, denominator, maturity, and suppression rules;
- promotion performance is not mistaken for retention;
- downloads are not mistaken for activation;
- opens alone are not return value;
- fewer than five subjects never produce exposed cohort metrics;
- interviews include retained, inactive, and privacy-concerned users;
- no notification, pricing, Persona, onboarding, and core-loop changes are
  combined during the same measured cohort;
- final evidence states uncertainty and does not overclaim small samples.

## Non-Goals

- guaranteeing App Store approval or organic acquisition;
- maximizing sessions, message count, or emotional dependency;
- private-content analytics or per-user admin timelines;
- adding unplanned features during launch;
- notification or payment experiments without separate approval;
- using physical ZEROON interest as digital retention evidence.

## Stop Rules

Pause promotion or expansion if record loss, cross-user access, consent
confusion, unsafe AI behavior, support failure, misleading store claims,
uncontained production incidents, or private-content evidence collection
appears.
